Summary

Cascade Bearing makes precision roller bearings for industrial gearboxes. In March 2025 its largest customer, representing 22% of revenue, moved to a second source. The stock fell 54% and has not recovered. I think the market has extrapolated a one-time contract loss into permanent impairment of a business whose economics have not changed.

At $18.40 the company trades at roughly 6x my estimate of normalized owner earnings, against a fifteen-year average of 13x. Replacement of the lost volume is already visible in the order book. I estimate fair value in the $34–42 range and expect the gap to close within 24 months as 2027 comparisons clean up.

Why the opportunity exists

What I could be wrong about

The single best discipline in this format: write the strongest version of the argument against yourself, and then say honestly whether you have an answer for it.
  • The customer never comes back. Why I think otherwise, and what evidence would change my mind.
  • Bearings get designed out. The technology risk, honestly stated.
  • Management does something dumb with the balance sheet. Capital allocation history and what I am watching.
